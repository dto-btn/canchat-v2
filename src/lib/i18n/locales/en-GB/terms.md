## Welcome to CANChat

CANChat is an advanced generative artificial intelligence solution provided by Shared Services Canada (SSC). It is designed to assist Government of Canada (GC) personnel in enhancing productivity, streamlining tasks, and improving communication.

By accessing, using, or interacting with CANChat, you, the user, acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms of Use, as well as all applicable Government of Canada policies, directives, and laws. **If you do not agree with these terms, you must not access or use CANChat.**

## 1. Definitions

- **CANChat:** Refers to the generative artificial intelligence solution, including its underlying models, user interface, features, and associated services.
- **User:** Any individual authorized to access and use CANChat.
- **Input Data:** Any text, documents, files, or information that a user provides or uploads to CANChat.
- **Output Content:** Any text, code, images, summaries, analyses, or other information generated or provided by CANChat in response to User Input Data.
- **Personal Information (PI):** Information about an identifiable individual that is recorded in any form. See section 3 of the https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/p-21/FullText.html for additional information.
- **Protected A:** Applies to information when unauthorized disclosure could reasonably be expected to cause limited or moderate injury outside the national interest, for example, disclosure of an exact salary figure.
- **Protected B:** Applies to information when unauthorized disclosure could reasonably be expected to cause serious injury outside the national interest, for example, loss of reputation or competitive advantage.
- **Protected C:** Applies to the very limited amount of information when unauthorized disclosure could reasonably be expected to cause extremely grave injury outside the national interest, for example, loss of life.
- **Sensitive Information or asset:** Information or asset that if compromised would reasonably be expected to cause an injury. This includes all information that falls within the exemption or exclusion criteria under the _Access to Information Act_ and the _Privacy Act_. This also includes controlled goods as well as other information and assets that have regulatory or statutory prohibitions and controls.
- **Aggregation:** Aggregation means a group of information resources or assets. The security category for information or asset repositories reflects the impact of aggregation, where more significant injury may occur when a group of information resources or assets is compromised. As a user of CANChat, users have a responsibility to use data appropriately in accordance with https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-privacy-playbook/how-to-use-share.html. This includes considering the aggregation and de-identification of data whenever possible.
- **Third-Party Providers:** External companies (e.g., Microsoft's OpenAI Service, Meta, Google, Cohere, etc.) that provide the Large Language Models (LLMs) leveraged by CANChat.
- **Transitory Record:** Transitory records are not of business value. They may include records that serve solely as convenience copies of records held in a government institution repository, but do not include any records that are required to control, support, or document the delivery of programs, to carry out operations, to make decisions, or to provide evidence to account for the activities of government at any time. (Source: https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/services/government/information-disposition/records/multi-institution-disposition-authorizations/2016-001-da-transitory-records.html).

### 5.1 Permitted Activities (for official GoC business only)

### 5.2 Prohibited Activities

You shall not, and shall not permit any third party to:

- **Input Data beyond Unclassified:** Do not use departmental information with a classification higher than Unclassified (e.g., Protected B, Protected C, Classified, Secret, Top Secret).
- **Prohibited Sensitive Information:** CANChat can only process Unclassified information. Users must not enter information that contains sensitive or private information into CANChat.
- **Text translation:** CANChat is not approved for translation services. As directed by the Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat (TBS) https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/policies-standards/policy-implementation-notice-departmental-adoption-gctranslate.html, GCtranslate is the default AI-assisted translation tool for Government of Canada institutions (up to Protected B) and must be used for translation activities where available.
- **Code Generation for Development or Production:**
  - **For Shared Services Canada Employees:** The use of CANChat or any other generative AI tool for generating, developing, or integrating code into a development or production setting is strictly prohibited due to intellectual property, copyright, and security vulnerability concerns.
  - **For Employees of Other Government of Canada Departments:** Before using CANChat for code generation, development, or integration, users are strongly encouraged to consult and comply with their department's specific policies, standards, and directives regarding the responsible use of generative AI for coding. Independent security review and Intellectual Property clearance are also highly recommended for any code intended for development or production.
- **Engage in Unlawful or Unethical Practices:** Do not use CANChat for any unlawful, unethical, or unauthorized purpose, or in any way that violates applicable laws or regulations. Please refer to the https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=25049.
- **Generate Harmful Content:** Do not generate harmful, offensive, malicious, discriminatory, hateful, or inappropriate content.
- **Bypass Security Measures:** Do not attempt to bypass departmental security measures, attempt to exploit vulnerabilities in CANChat or associated systems, or engage in any form of unauthorized access.
- **Impersonate:** Do not impersonate any person or entity, or falsely state or otherwise misrepresent your affiliation with a person or entity.
- **Automated Decision-Making:** Do not use CANChat for automated decision-making that directly affects individuals without comprehensive human review, validation, and adherence to the Directive on Automated Decision-Making.
- **Commercial Use:** Do not use CANChat for any commercial purpose unrelated to official Government of Canada business.

## 6. Employee Responsibilities & Limitations of AI

Employees must understand and be aware of certain inherent limitations that exist with generative AI services. When using services such as CANChat to craft documents or responses that will be used to inform decision-makers, it is imperative to validate the outputs generated by these services for accuracy, clarity, completeness, and relevance. Therefore, when used in an official capacity, employees are responsible to:

- **Awareness of Data Timeliness:** Recognize that the effectiveness and currency of AI models are influenced by the timeliness and age of their training data. Output Content may not reflect the most current information or policies.
- **Ethical Considerations & Bias:** Be aware of potential ethical concerns, including the possibility of inherent biases or inaccuracies in AI-generated content due to biases in training data. Ensure that its application aligns with the organization's values and goals and actively work to identify and mitigate such biases.
- **Human Oversight & Verification:** Act as the ultimate subject matter expert and are solely responsible for verifying the accuracy, completeness, and suitability of all Output Content before relying on it or disseminating it.
- **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** Be aware of and comply with all legal requirements, privacy regulations (_Privacy Act_), and intellectual property implications when working with CANChat Input or Output content.
- **Responsible Disclosure:** Clearly identify when content is generated with assistance from CANChat. This helps maintain accountability, facilitates understanding of the AI-generated results by stakeholders, promotes trust, and mitigates potential risks (e.g., by adding a disclaimer like "This content was generated with assistance from CANChat and validated by a human").
- **Risk Mitigation:** Understand the potential risks associated with generative AI, such as the creation of misleading content, amplification of biases, or generation of "hallucinations" (plausible but incorrect information).

## 9. Data Privacy and Handling

- **Data Collection:** SSC collects and stores your Input Data, Output Content, interaction logs, usage metrics, and technical information to operate, monitor, secure, and improve CANChat.
- **Purpose of Collection:** This data is collected to:
  - Provide and maintain the CANChat service.
  - Monitor for compliance with these Terms of Use and relevant Government of Canada policies.
  - Ensure the security and integrity of the system.
  - Debug and improve the performance and functionality of CANChat.
  - Generate anonymized and aggregated statistical reports for service management.
  - Crucially, Input Data and Output Content will not be used for training the underlying generative AI models.
- **Sharing with Third-Party Providers:** By using CANChat, you understand that your Input Data and interactions with CANChat will be processed by the Third-Party Providers (e.g., Azure OpenAI Service, Cohere, Meta, Google) you select through CANChat. Only Unclassified information may be entered into CANChat in accordance with these Terms of Use. This processing is subject to SSC's agreements with these providers, which **explicitly state that user data will not be used for model training.**
- **Data Storage:** User data (Input Data, Output Content, logs) will be stored on Government of Canada approved infrastructure and handled in accordance with the security classification of the data.
- **Data Retention:** All chat data (Input Data, Output Content, interaction logs for individual chats) is retained for **30 days from the last activity in each chat**. Other usage metrics and aggregated statistical reports may be retained for longer periods in accordance with SSC's information management policies and schedules.
- **Personal Information:** Users must not enter Personal Information into CANChat. Shared Services Canada is committed to protecting information collected as part of the operation of the service in accordance with the Privacy Act and the https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/doc-eng.aspx?id=18309.
- **Monitoring:** You acknowledge that all usage of CANChat, including Input Data and Output Content, is subject to monitoring and audit for security, compliance, and investigative purposes. There is no expectation of privacy regarding your use of CANChat.
- **Business Continuity:** SSC's business continuity approach focuses on (1) operational recovery, which restores service availability without recovering user data, and (2) full recovery, which restores both service and data. Users of CANChat should be aware that operational recovery will be prioritized to re-establish core functionality quickly. Transitory records (ex. transient sessions, caches, temporary indexes) will be recovered on a best-effort basis.
